# Note to the BWC committee: services to endorsed brands

Two documents. The internal circulation note goes to Chad, Feroza and
Zenith first. The committee note goes to BWC only after they have seen it.

This is the note promised in the 12 September internal email "After the
17th", under "What has to happen before we sell anything". The site
launched on 17 September. Until this is answered, Omni's own rule is
"prepare everything, approach nobody", which is holding 165 warm names
closed.

## One thing to weigh on timing

The committee is currently working through a BWC investment matter and a
SARS review, and a bank letter is going back and forth. That is not a
reason to sit on this, but it is a reason to keep the note short, clearly
separable from that business, and easy to answer in one line. If the team
thinks it lands badly this week, the cost of waiting a week is one week of
Tier 1, and the Tier 0 listing work carries on regardless.

**To:** Toni, Janneke, Laureen, Carol, Heather, Zaahira, Beryl
**Copy:** Chad Cupido, for recusal, not for decision
**From:** Tumelo Ncube, Omni Wellness Media
**Subject:** Humane Guide: one decision I need from the committee, on services to endorsed brands

Dear committee,

Chad is copied on this but is not part of the decision. He is BWC's
Executive Officer and Omni's founder, and the only clean way to handle that
is for him to be visibly out of the room. I am asking you directly.

**What is already agreed.** From launch, Omni manages the Humane Guide:
the listings, the onboarding of newly endorsed brands, the social and the
marketing. That is the mandate and it is not what this note is about.

**What I am asking you to decide.** Whether Omni may offer commercial
services to brands that are endorsed by BWC, and if so, whether BWC says
anything about it or stays silent.

**Why I am asking rather than assuming.** Many endorsed brands need
websites, photography and social media, which is what Omni does. We are
about to be in regular contact with all of them because we maintain their
listings. That is an obvious commercial opportunity and it is also an
obvious conflict, so it should be your call and not mine.

**The wall I propose, whatever you decide.**

Everything on BWC's property is free, equal, and not for sale. A complete
and accurate listing, a good logo, a proper description, correct
categories, working links. Every endorsed brand gets that as part of the
endorsement they already hold. No brand can buy more of it. No brand gets
less for declining anything.

Everything on a brand's own property, their website, their photography,
their social media, is ordinary commercial work at Omni's published rates,
entirely voluntary, and declining it changes nothing about the listing.

Three commitments that follow, which I would like minuted:

1. Omni's commercial relationship with a brand can never touch that
   brand's endorsement. Not whether they are endorsed, not how they are
   listed, not their position, not their data.
2. BWC may audit Omni's conduct here at any time, without notice.
3. Omni will never state or imply that BWC endorses its services. BWC
   endorses cruelty-free products. It does not endorse web design.

**The three answers open to you, all of which work for us.**

- **Neutral.** Omni may offer services, BWC says nothing either way, and
  no BWC channel is used to introduce them.
- **Listed supplier.** BWC names Omni as one supplier that endorsed brands
  may use, alongside others, with the wall above published.
- **No.** Omni does not sell to endorsed brands at all while it manages
  the Guide.

I have a preference for the first, because it is the easiest to explain to
anyone who asks. I will implement whichever you choose, including the
third.

**What is happening in the meantime.** Listing repair work is already
under way, because it is the job and it is free to every brand. The audit
so far has found eight listings showing another company's logo, 44 percent
needing artwork, eight dead or stale links, seven with no website recorded
and four pointing at a social page. None of that waits on this decision.
No brand has been approached commercially and none will be until you have
answered.

Two listings need a decision from you rather than a repair, and I will send
those separately: one points at a United States multi-level marketing
company, and one has no page on its own parent's site.

I am asking for an answer at the next committee meeting, or by email if
that is quicker. The value of the Humane Guide is that it cannot be
bought. I would rather be slow and obviously clean about this than fast.

Thank you,

Tumelo Ncube
Omni Wellness Media
