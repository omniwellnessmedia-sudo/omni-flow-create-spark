# Sales actions the team can take this week

Written 17 September 2026. Nothing here needs the Google Ads account, which
is suspended, or any budget.

## The honest starting position

The pipeline shows about 75 leads. Most of that number is not sales.

| What | How many | Is it a sales lead? |
|---|---|---|
| Real site enquiries and contact form submissions | roughly 8 | Yes. Work these first. |
| Sponsorship and grant prospects imported 7 May 2026 | 67 | No, not as they stand |

The 67 were gathered for a different job: asking corporates and foundations
to fund programme work. Old Mutual, MTN, Sanlam Grants, DG Murray Trust, UN
Women, the Raith Foundation. Eight of them are marked positive or awaiting,
which looks like warm sales leads on the board and is not. Those are open
funding applications, and one of them, the Shoprite one, even carries a
reference number. Working them as sales prospects would confuse a funding
relationship with a customer one and would probably lose both.

So: do not sell to the funders. There is, though, one part of that list that
is genuinely commercial, and it is below.

## 1. The eight real enquiries, today

People who came to the site and asked. Nothing else in the pipeline is
warmer than someone who already put their hand up.

Admin, Sales, Pipeline, filter to Enquiries and Contact form. For each one:
reply, move the card, set a follow-up. If any is older than a week, say so
in the reply rather than pretending it is fresh.

Owner: one named person, today. This is the single highest value hour
available to the team this week.

## 2. Sixteen Cape Town businesses Omni has already spoken to

The hospitality and training cluster in the imported list is real commercial
market for what Omni sells: media production, web, social. Several are on
the same stretch of coast as the Muizenberg plan.

International Hotel School, Infinity Culinary Training, Capsicum Culinary
Studio, Plant Cafe, Wild Eatery, Wildsprout, Romeo and Vero, Taj Hotel,
Southern Sun Hotel, Hotel Verde, Cape Grace Hotel, False Bay TVET College,
Tigers Milk Muizenberg, Olympia Cafe and Bakery, Knead Bakery, City
Sightseeing Cape Town.

All sixteen have a working email address on file. All were contacted in
early 2026, about sponsorship, not about buying anything.

**This has to be an honest second conversation, not a follow-up to the
first.** They were asked to give. They are now being offered something to
buy. Say that plainly in the first line, or it reads as a bait and switch:

> Earlier this year we approached you about supporting a community
> programme. This is a separate note and a different subject. Omni Wellness
> Media is a media and marketing company in Muizenberg, and [one sentence
> on the specific thing you noticed about their site or listing].

Four more in that cluster declined the sponsorship ask: Radisson Red,
Woolworths MySchool, Chef Skills Development, Peermont. Declining a donation
request is not declining a service. They are worth approaching later, but
not in the first batch, and not without acknowledging the earlier no.

A ready CSV of the sixteen is in the scratchpad next to this file, in the
shape the importer takes.

## 3. The Muizenberg walk, which needs nobody's permission

Already built and waiting: the page at /muizenberg, the printable sheet at
/muizenberg/audit-sheet, and walk-in capture on the Pipeline.

The method is the product. Spend five minutes on a business's website and
Google profile, write three specific findings, walk in, hand over the sheet,
leave it with them whether or not they buy. Two entry offers at published
prices to close on.

Decide in the meeting: who walks, which days, how many doors. Ten doors a
day beats a plan for a hundred.

## 4. Load a list and work it

New on the Pipeline: **Import a list**. Paste from a spreadsheet or a CSV,
name the list, set a follow-up date, and the rows land in New as leads. It
shows you what it will do before it does it, skips anything already on the
board, and refuses a row with no business name rather than inventing one.

That means a list built any honest way becomes a worked pipeline in one
paste. Walking a road and writing names down. The businesses already in
someone's phone. A trade association's public member list. What it must not
become is a scraped or bought list: under POPIA that is a problem, and a
cold blast to addresses nobody gave you is also simply bad selling for a
business whose case is that it does careful work.

Give every list a name you will recognise in a month, like
`muizenberg-cafes-sep`, so the Channels view can tell you which lists
actually produced revenue.

## 5. What to say

Two entry offers exist at published prices, and prices come from
`src/data/publicRateCard.ts`, never from memory. Lead with the finding, not
the offer. "Your booking link has been broken since June" opens a
conversation. "We do web development" does not.

- The Muizenberg walk: who, which days, how many doors.
- Whether the 67 sponsorship contacts get archived out of the sales board so
