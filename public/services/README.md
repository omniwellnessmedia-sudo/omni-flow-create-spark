# Service page imagery

Drop photographs for /services and /services/<slug> in here.

## What to upload

Originals are fine. Camera files at 4MB, 9MB or 17MB are expected and do
not need to be shrunk first: they get resized to about 1600px wide and
converted to WebP before they are referenced anywhere, which takes each to
roughly 150 to 250KB. Uploading an unprocessed 17MB JPEG straight onto a
hero would be punishing on a phone on mobile data, so the conversion step
is not optional.

## Before a photograph of a person goes on this page

/services sells things. A photograph of an identifiable person on a page
that sells things is a different matter from the same photograph in an
event gallery, and this repository already carries one standing rule of
that kind: see the note in src/lib/images.ts about a group photo that had
to be repointed because one person must not appear on the site.

So for any Omni photograph of real people, confirm first:

- who is in it, and that each of them is cleared for commercial use
- that nobody in it is a minor, or if they are, that written guardian
  consent exists for this use

Stock photography carries none of these questions, which is why the
royalty-free set is the faster route to something live.

## Naming

Lowercase, hyphens, no spaces. The band it belongs to makes a good prefix:
clarity-, build-, content-, retainer-, podcast-, campaign-.
