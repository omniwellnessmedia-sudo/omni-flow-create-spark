How to run the screen

One deck, one operator. Right arrow advances one cue. Every honouree is a single keypress: the reveal animation plays on arrival, roughly one second, then the slide holds until you advance.

Cue numbers below match the slide numbers in the deck. If you need to jump, press the number keys or click the slide in the left-hand rail.

Press R to return to the holding slide. Do this at 13:45 and leave it up while the house fills.

Before the doors open

  1. Open Ceremony Screen Deck.html full screen on the projector output. Check the holding slide fills the screen with no browser chrome.
  2. Step through three honouree slides and confirm the cards load. A missing card shows a dashed placeholder with the name — that is safe to project but tells you an image is still outstanding.
  3. Confirm the certificate table stage right is stacked in the same order as this sheet.
  4. Agree the timekeeper's cue with the MC. Two minutes per honouree.

The timing model

Two minutes per honouree is what makes ninety minutes work. At three minutes the ceremony runs twelve minutes over before anything else moves.

It holds because roughly twelve honourees are present in person; the rest receive by representative, remotely or in absentia, and those cues run shorter.

Individual photographs happen after the ceremony, not during it. This is agreed in advance so nobody is rushed on the day by a decision they did not hear about.

Movement on stage

  1. Reserved honouree seating, front two rows, house right.
  2. One-way route: up the stage-right steps, receive at the award zone, photograph at the mark, exit stage right.
  3. Nobody crosses the MC position at stage left.
  4. Certificate table stage right, just off the award zone, stacked in presentation order, one named owner.
  5. Fixed photographer position, front of house centre.
  6. Timekeeper front row centre, with a visible cue to the MC.

The screen sits behind the award zone, which puts the branding in every photograph. Keep it as it is.
