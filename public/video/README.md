# Video for the public pages

Drop video files in here and I will place them.

## What to upload

The original export is fine. I re-encode before anything is referenced:
web-size MP4 (H.264) plus WebM where it helps, a poster frame so the page
has something to show before the video loads, and dimensions declared so
the layout cannot jump around it.

## Size is the whole problem with video

A photograph that is too heavy costs a visitor a second. A video that is
too heavy costs them the page. On South African mobile data an autoplaying
20MB background loop is a real expense to a real person, and it is charged
to them, not to us.

So:

- Under 30 seconds for anything that plays automatically behind a heading.
- Under 3MB after encoding for the same. I will get it there; send the
  original and let me do the compressing.
- Longer pieces are fine as click-to-play with a poster frame. They only
  download when somebody chooses them.
- Anything over about 50MB as an original should probably go to YouTube or
  Vimeo and be embedded instead, because a file that size in the repository
  is downloaded by everyone who clones it forever.

## Sound

Anything that plays on its own plays muted, always, and carries a visible
control. A page that makes noise at somebody in an open-plan office is a
page they close.

## Before a video of real people goes on a page that sells

Same rule as the photography in ../services:

- who is in it, and that each of them is cleared for commercial use
- that nobody in it is a minor, or if they are, that written guardian
  consent exists for this use

## Naming

Lowercase, hyphens, no spaces. Say where it goes: home-hero.mp4,
services-intro.mp4, screenings-recap.mp4.
